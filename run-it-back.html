Questions
How many games will my team play?
Every team is guaranteed at least 2 pool games. Teams that advance play 3 or 4 games total — 2 in pool play, then semis, and the final if you make it.
How do teams advance from pool play?
Most wins advance. Each team plays 2 pool games — win both and you're through. Tied on wins? We look at point differential: add up your scoring margin across both games. The team with the bigger combined margin advances. Still exactly tied? Coin flip.
What happens if a game is tied at full time?
In pool play, ties count as a loss for both teams in terms of advancement — you need a win to advance. In the bracket (semis and final), if the game is tied at 10 minutes, play continues in sudden death — next score wins.
How long are the games?
Each game is 10 minutes with a 5-minute break between games. The clock runs continuously with no timeouts. Games start on time to keep the schedule moving.
How many players per team?
3 players on court at a time. You can bring subs — just confirm your full roster at check-in by 2:30 pm.
What if a team doesn't show up?
Teams that forfeit receive a loss. Their opponents take the win. Please arrive by 2:30 pm so we can start on time.
