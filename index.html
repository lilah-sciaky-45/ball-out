The Community

Ball Out Portland is a queer community-run basketball group centering queer women, trans, non-binary, and gender expansive players and their people.

Weekly Pickup

Every Saturday. Half and full court runs. Queer & friends. Intermediate level. Outdoors when possible, indoors when it rains. Size 6 ball.

Who Can Play

Queer women, trans, non-binary, gender expansive ballers and allies. Show up solo or with friends. Good vibes strictly enforced.

Cost

Free when outdoors. $5 when indoors at Charles Jordan Community Center. No one turned away.
