What Went
Down

The Event

Run It Back was a 3v3 tournament and party brought to you by Ball Out Portland and Let Her Cook DJ collective. Pool play followed by semis and a final. 8 teams. One champion.

Date & Time

Saturday, May 2nd, 2026
Check-in: 2:30PM
Tip-off: 3PM

Location

WAREHOUSE
330 SE 6th Ave
Portland, Oregon

Prizes

1st and 2nd place prizes. MVP & Baller Outfit awards.

Rules

Standard 3v3 rules. If you liked a call, you tipped your ref. Good vibes strictly enforced.

Who Came

Everyone (21+). Ball Out Portland is a queer community-run basketball group centering queer women, trans, non-binary, and gender expansive players and their people.
